# How to: rate / 如何进行分级

> Source: [Danbooru wiki - howto:rate](https://danbooru.donmai.us/wiki_pages/howto:rate)

> Ratings are used to categorize content on Danbooru. Danbooru has four ratings: rating:general, rating:sensitive,
> rating:questionable, and rating:explicit.
> **分级用于对 Danbooru 上的内容进行分类。Danbooru 共有四个分级：rating:general（一般级）、rating:sensitive（敏感级）、rating:
> questionable（存疑级）和 rating:explicit（限制级）。**

> Broadly speaking, these categories are:
> **概括来说，这些分类分别是：**

- rating:general - G-rated content. Completely safe for work. Nothing sexualized or inappropriate to view in front of
  others. Trivial or cartoon violence.
- **rating:general —— G 级内容。完全适合在工作场合观看。没有任何性暗示或不宜在他人面前观看的内容。仅限轻微或卡通式的暴力。**

- rating:sensitive - Ecchi, sexy, suggestive, or mildly erotic. Skimpy or revealing clothes, swimsuits, underwear,
  images focused on the breasts or ass, and anything else potentially not safe for work. Mild violence.
- **rating:sensitive ——
  擦边（Ecchi）、性感、挑逗或轻度情色的内容。包括暴露或凸显身材的衣着、泳装、内衣、聚焦于胸部或臀部的图像，以及其他任何可能不适合在工作场合观看的内容。轻度暴力。**

- rating:questionable - Softcore erotica and/or graphic violence. Simple nudity or near-nudity, but no explicit sex or
  exposed genitals.
- **rating:questionable —— 软色情和／或露骨的暴力画面。单纯的裸露或近乎全裸，但不包含明确的性行为或暴露的生殖器。**

- rating:explicit - Hardcore erotica and/or extremely graphic violence. Explicit sex acts, exposed genitals, and bodily
  fluids.
- **rating:explicit —— 硬核色情和／或极度血腥的暴力画面。包括明确的性行为、暴露的生殖器以及体液。**

## Guidelines / 分级准则

### Rating:General / 一般级（G）

- G-rated, completely safe for work content.

- Content without people (scenery, still lifes, no humans, text-only pages, food focus, animal focus, mecha, SFW
  Pokemon, etc)
- **不含人物的内容（风景、静物、无人类、纯文字页面、以食物为焦点、以动物为焦点、机甲、健全的宝可梦作品等）。**

- Innocent displays of affection (hugging, holding hands, kissing that isn't sexually charged, etc)
- **单纯的亲昵表现（拥抱、牵手、不带有性意味的亲吻等）。**

- Trivial or cartoon violence (comedic or non-serious fighting, minor head bumps, cuts, blood, or bruises, or other

- Legal drug use (smoking or drinking alcohol).
- **合法的成瘾品使用（吸烟或饮酒）。**

- Mild profanity, insults, or rude gestures (e.g. middle finger) without lewd or sexual connotations.
- **轻度的粗话、辱骂或无礼手势（例如竖中指），且不带有下流或性暗示的意味。**

- Note that anything involving child-like characters that is the least bit inappropriate or suggestive should not be
  rated G.
- **请注意：任何涉及孩童般角色的内容，只要有丝毫不得体或暗示性，就不得评为 G 级。**
